Catalog

Products

Everything you make: a photo, the filaments it takes, what it costs at your prices, and how many are on the shelf. Tap one to edit it, make more, or sell some.

Filament

Your rack

Every spool you own, rolls on hand and grams left on the open roll. Tap a spool to receive stock, adjust what is left, or take it off the rack.

Rolls on hand counts the open roll. The bar under each spool is the open roll; a spool turns red when what is left falls under its reorder point.

Add stock

The color wall

Every Bambu Lab PLA color, grouped by line. Set what you pay per roll in each line's header, then tap a spool to put it on your rack.

Swatches use Bambu Lab's published display hex codes. Dawn Radiance, South Beach and Mystic Magenta are approximations. The price in each line header is what every spool in that line costs unless you give a spool its own price.

History

Activity

Everything that moved stock: rolls received, products made and sold, adjustments. Undo puts the stock back.

Restock

Reorder list

Spools under their reorder point and products below their shelf minimum.

Scanning

Labels

Print a barcode for each spool and product. Scanned, a label does whatever the scan bar is set to: receive, make, sell, or look up. Bambu boxes already have a barcode; the first scan of a new one asks which color it is.
